The Difference Between Camera Traps and Flock Cameras - September 2026

Lael Gilbert, Public Relations Lead at Utah State University, wrote a story on the importance of using camera traps for wildlife research, and how these cameras differ from Flock cameras in a number of ways. Read the official article on Utah State Today.

Swaner Ecocenter Webinar - February 2026

Dr. David Stoner conducted a webinar through Swaner Preserve and Ecocenter called "The Moose Next Door." This webinar covers the natural history of moose, safety tips, and an introduction to our project. View the webinar on Swaner's YouTube channel.
