Frequently Asked Questions

1. Who is conducting this study? Utah State University.


2. Why is there a camera on the trail? We are conducting a wildlife-recreation study to determine when and where recreators are most at risk of having conflict with a moose.


3. What kind of data are you capturing? We are capturing wildlife data (species identified, male/female, etc.), the number of people in each group of recreators, and what kind of recreation is happening (biking, hiking, on-leash dog walking, etc.).


4. Who can view the images? Only the immediate research team can view the images.


5. How is my privacy being protected? We follow a strict protocol that requires us to put all images through a blurring software to fully blur any humans captured, and immediately delete all originals. We are not logging any identifiable information. This is a scientific research project, and no photos may be used for law enforcement. Our data protocols have been reviewed and approved by the Institutional Review Board. See below photo of Melany on the trail, after the blurring software has been used.
