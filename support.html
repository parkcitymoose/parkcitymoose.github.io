Monetary Donations

Our research operates on a limited budget, and maintaining a long-term camera trap study requires a steady supply of field equipment and materials. With cameras deployed across two counties, cost for field supplies can add up quickly. We are incredibly grateful for any support that helps us keep our project running. Financial contributions can help cover the ongoing costs of fieldwork, including batteries, camera equipment, and materials used for outreach and education.
